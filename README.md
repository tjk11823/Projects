## Featured Work

### [Howmet Aerospace (NYSE: HWM) – Institutional Equity Research & Valuation](./Howmet_Aerospace_Equity_Research_Price_Target.pdf)
* Overview: An in-depth institutional stock pitch and equity research report. The core investment thesis evaluates recent market reactions to perceived industry shifts, such as SpaceX's internal foundry developments and GE Aerospace's acquisition of CPP, and contrasts them with the company's established structural moats, including a ~90% market share in rotating aero-engine castings and high multi-year FAA certification barriers.
* Valuation & Modeling: Constructed a comprehensive Discounted Cash Flow (DCF) valuation model featuring a 3-case scenario analysis (Bull, Base, Bear). The model stress-tests revenue growth and EBITDA margins against OEM supply chain bottlenecks (Boeing 737 MAX, Airbus A320neo delays) and aftermarket mix-shifts.
* Technical Execution: Reconciled historical financial data from SEC filings, calculated WACC, and derived exit multiples to establish a fundamentally grounded price target.
