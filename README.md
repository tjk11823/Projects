## Featured Work

### [Howmet Aerospace (NYSE: HWM) – Institutional Equity Research & Valuation](./Howmet_Aerospace_Equity_Research_Price_Target.pdf)
* Overview: An in-depth institutional stock pitch and equity research report. The core investment thesis evaluates recent market reactions to perceived industry shifts, such as SpaceX's internal foundry developments and GE Aerospace's acquisition of CPP, and contrasts them with the company's established structural moats, including a ~90% market share in rotating aero-engine castings and high multi-year FAA certification barriers.
* Valuation & Modeling: Constructed a comprehensive Discounted Cash Flow (DCF) valuation model, **[Download Excel DCF Valuation Model](./Howmet_Excel_Tables.xlsx):** Complete 3-case scenario framework establishing the ~20% upside target and 30.x Base Case exit multiple, featuring a 3-case scenario analysis (Bull, Base, Bear). The model stress-tests revenue growth and EBITDA margins against OEM supply chain bottlenecks (Boeing 737 MAX, Airbus A320neo delays) and aftermarket mix-shifts.
* Technical Execution: Reconciled historical financial data from SEC filings, calculated WACC, and derived exit multiples to establish a fundamentally grounded price target.
### [Quantitative Equity Portfolio Prospectus & Macroeconomic Analysis](./Fidelity_Portfolio_Prospectus.pdf)
* Overview: A comprehensive long-only equity and fixed-income portfolio prospectus with a 1-year fund term. The core investment thesis evaluates current macroeconomic conditions to identify fundamentally mispriced assets, driving an aggressive overweight allocation in AI-levered mega-cap technology and semiconductor equities for the near term due to lower multiple valuations. 
* Strategy: Overweight U.S. Technology stocks, while risk-budgetting using T-Bills, Consumper Staples (ETF), and Aerospace and Defense to prevent overexposure to downturns in the near-term. These dilute the beta and allow for better principal preservation
* Technical Execution: Integrated top-down macroeconomic forecasting with bottom-up fundamental analysis, utilizing robust Discounted Cash Flow (DCF) models, P/E valuations, and strict asset-liability matching to actively manage capital requirements and structural drawdowns.
### [Independent Research Paper: High-Frequency Identification of Monetary Policy and Information Shocks Across Implied Equity Durations](./Independent_Research_Paper.pdf)
* **Overview:** An empirical macroeconomic research paper investigating the heterogeneous impact of FOMC communications on equity markets. The research disentangles pure monetary policy shocks from central bank information shocks, demonstrating that information shocks significantly reprice long-duration equities, whereas short-term rate surprises fail to meaningfully alter long-run discount rates.
* **Methodology:** Utilized the USMPD high-frequency event study database (examining 30-minute windows around FOMC announcements) and calculated implied equity durations over a 15-year finite forecasting horizon. Designed cross-sectional regression models evaluating CAPM cumulative abnormal returns (CAR) across 20 duration quantiles with beta interaction terms.
